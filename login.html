How log in is protected

Each rule links to a requirement in the SRS, Section 6.

Same message every time. A wrong password and an unknown account both show "Invalid credentials" (SEC-04).
Blocked after 5 failures. Five failed attempts in 15 minutes block log in for 15 minutes (SEC-07).
Secure session. A new session ID is issued on log in, in an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite cookie (SEC-05).
Passwords are hashed. They are never stored or logged in plain text (SEC-03).
